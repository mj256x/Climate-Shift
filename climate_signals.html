The signals are
already visible.

Our planet is sending clear warning signs.
These changes are happening now—and they affect the natural world, our communities,
and our future.

Rising temperatures

Global temperatures are climbing, driving heatwaves, shifting seasons, and putting extra stress on people, wildlife, and ecosystems.

Melting ice

Glaciers and sea ice are shrinking fast. Loss of ice speeds up warming and threatens water supplies and wildlife habitats.

Higher seas

As oceans rise, coastal flooding, erosion, and saltwater intrusion are becoming more frequent and more damaging.

Extreme weather

Stronger storms, heavier rains, droughts, and wildfires are happening more often and causing greater destruction.

Ecosystem disruption

Changing conditions are throwing off delicate balances, threatening species and the natural systems we depend on.

Why it matters!

These signals aren't abstract—they show up in our daily lives and in our communities.

Threats to clean air, safe water, and healthy food.

Risks to our health, homes, and livelihoods.

Greater strain on communities—especially those least responsible for the problem.

Loss of nature's beauty, diversity, and resilience.
